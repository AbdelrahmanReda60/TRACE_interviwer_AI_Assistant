Human-in-the-Loop Scope & Safeguards: TRACE is an interactive evaluation tool used after ATS filtering to assist a human interviewer. It compares one remaining candidate's CV against human-confirmed job requirements, displays exact citations, and suggests optional follow-up questions. “No evidence found in CV” indicates absence of written documentation, not absence of skill. TRACE does not rank candidates, calculate automated rejection scores, or make hiring decisions.

How to Present TRACE (Classroom Run of Show)

Follow this 5-step walkthrough during the Week 4 check-in to demonstrate the core Human-AI Interaction (HAI) principles:

Step 1 Explain Scope & Post-ATS Framing

Point out the top scope banner: TRACE operates after initial ATS filtering for a single remaining candidate. Emphasize that TRACE does not rank candidates, reject applicants, or make hiring decisions.

Step 2 Inspect Evidence Coverage & Disclaimers

Show the prominent Evidence Coverage metric (e.g. 60% for C-01). Explain that it is defined strictly as (Found ÷ Total) × 100. Point to the clear disclaimer: it describes documentation in the CV, never candidate ability or hiring fitness.

Step 3 Inspect Exact Source Excerpts vs Ambiguity

Click on Python programming (Found) to show the exact cited excerpt from Projects · p.1. Then click on Cloud deployment (Needs verification) or Team leadership (No evidence found in CV) to demonstrate how TRACE surfaces uncertainty instead of hallucinating.

Step 4 Human Control & Question Builder

Show the optional follow-up interview question. Edit the wording and click “Add question to notes” to prepare your interview guide.

Step 5 Override Evidence Label & Live Recalculation

Use the Human Oversight control to change a status (e.g., change Cloud to “Evidence found”). Point out how the Evidence Coverage percentage and the Strengths/Gaps summary immediately recalculate dynamically!
